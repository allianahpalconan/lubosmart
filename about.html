About LubosMart

Building a better, closer way to shop with your neighborhood.

LubosMart started with a simple idea: neighborhood shopping shouldn't disappear just because more of life moved online. We built a marketplace where local Filipino sellers can reach their community with the same convenience as any big platform — without losing what makes buying local worth it.

Every seller on LubosMart is verified, every order is tracked from checkout to doorstep, and every peso spent supports a shop in your own neighborhood.
